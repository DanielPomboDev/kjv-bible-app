# Demo video script — "KJV Bible in 60 seconds"

Target: 60-second silent captioned tour for YouTube. No narration needed;
captions carry it. Record at 1920×1080, publish at 720p.

## Shots

| # | Seconds | What's on screen | Caption |
|---|---------|------------------|---------|
| 1 | 0–7 | Reading pane, Genesis 1 | KJV Bible — free, offline, no account. |
| 2 | 7–16 | Ctrl+K, type "faith", results appear | Search any word, phrase, or reference like John 3:16. |
| 3 | 16–28 | Ctrl+click 3 verses, selection pill | Select verses: click, Ctrl+click, Shift+click — then Add to Deck. |
| 4 | 28–38 | Open the sermon deck panel | Your deck: reorder, notes, backgrounds. |
| 5 | 38–44 | Click Present → single-display warning | One click presents fullscreen. |
| 6 | 44–55 | Stage + presenter window | The congregation sees slides. You see notes. |
| 7 | 55–62 | Back at the reading pane (Esc) | Free forever — download link below. |
